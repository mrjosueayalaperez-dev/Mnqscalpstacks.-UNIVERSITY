Honest labels · Etiquetas honestas. Voice: your device's own English/Spanish speech (the same voices the University uses today). Mouth movement: phoneme-estimated (Tier 1) — English uses the engine's official module; Spanish uses a first custom module written for this prototype, because no free Spanish module exists. True phonetic sync is the Tier 2 step (pre-recorded lesson audio). The 3D model is a CC0 example avatar (MakeHuman/MPFB via the TalkingHead project) — a placeholder body for evaluating motion and presence; the production face and wardrobe are a separate authoring step. Engine: TalkingHead (MIT) + Three.js. Nothing on this page is connected to the live University.
