Part I — Our Official Mission Statement

Our Mission

To make professional-quality futures trading education accessible to anyone willing to learn, practice, and develop discipline.

We believe financial education should not be reserved for those who can afford expensive courses or trading systems.

Our mission is to give beginners the foundation to understand financial markets, manage risk, recognize opportunities, and make independent decisions.

We teach through structured lessons, interactive charts, market simulations, practical exercises, and measurable progress.

We do not promise profits.
We do not sell dreams of overnight wealth.
We teach preparation, patience, accountability, and disciplined execution.

Our Five Foundational Principles
  1. I
    Education Before ExecutionUnderstand the market before risking capital.
  2. II
    Discipline Before ProfitA successful decision is one that follows a sound process — regardless of the outcome of any individual trade.
  3. III
    Risk Before RewardProtecting capital is essential to remaining in the market long enough to learn.
  4. IV
    Practice Before PerformanceStudents demonstrate understanding through simulation, journaling, and review.
  5. V
    Knowledge Before EgoWhat we learn is meant to be shared, improved, and passed forward.

We don't chase burning candles.
We wait for the retest.
We don't gamble on hope.
We trade with discipline.
We don't hide what we've learned.
We teach those who come behind us.

Part II — The Legacy Page

Welcome to M&Q University

To the student who comes after us —

Welcome.

Perhaps you've never traded a futures contract. Perhaps you've lost money trying to understand the markets. Perhaps you've watched charts move up and down without knowing why. Or perhaps you're simply curious, and willing to learn something new.

Wherever you're starting from, you belong in a classroom where questions are encouraged and progress comes through practice.

How This University Began

M&Q University began with one person determined to understand the financial markets.

There was no perfect strategy. No guarantee of success. Only questions, charts, mistakes, practice, and the willingness to keep learning.

What began as a personal journey gradually became something bigger. A trading dashboard became an educational laboratory. The laboratory became a structured curriculum. And the curriculum became a university built around one belief:

Knowledge becomes more valuable when it is shared.

A Message From the Founder

I started learning because I wanted to understand something I knew very little about.

Along the way, I discovered that learning requires patience, discipline, and the courage to admit when you are wrong.

I made mistakes. I asked questions. I kept practicing.

Eventually, I realized that the knowledge I was gaining could help someone else. That became the purpose of this university.

I don't want this knowledge to stop with me. I want the next student to learn from our experiences, improve upon our work, and pass that knowledge forward.

If you become a better student, a more disciplined trader, or a more thoughtful decision-maker because of something you learned here, then this university has fulfilled its purpose.

Knowledge is the legacy.

— Founder, MNQ Scalp Stacks
Part III — Our University Pledge

Before You Begin

Before beginning their education, every student encounters this pledge:

The M&Q University Student Pledge

I commit to learning before risking.
I will respect the markets and understand that losses are part of trading.
I will practice patience instead of chasing opportunities.
I will protect my capital through responsible risk management.
I will measure my progress through discipline, preparation, and improvement.
I will never confuse luck with skill.
And when I gain knowledge, I will remember those who helped me learn.
